Chicken Adobo

Adobo is the ultimate Filipino comfort food. A rich, savory-tangy dish where tender pork, chicken, or vegetables are slow-braised in a luscious reduction of vinegar, soy sauce, garlic, bay leaves, and black peppercorns. Equal parts salty, sour, and garlic-loaded, it's a centerpiece of Philippine culinary culture.

Ingredients

  • Chicken: 1 kg
  • Soy Sauce: 1/2 cup:
  • Vinegar: 1/2 cup:
  • Minced Garlic: 1 head :
  • Peppercorns: 1 tsp whole black peppercorns
  • Dried Bay Leaves: 2-3 pieces:
  • Water: 1 cup
