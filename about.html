About Jedidiah

ᰔ Mabuhay! I am Jedidiah Baltazar ᰔ

Hi! I’m the person behind Mangan, a little corner dedicated to the Filipino food I grew up loving.

My love for cooking started at home, where many of the recipes I know came from my mom and my grandma. From watching them prepare familiar Filipino dishes to enjoying their food around the family table, I learned that food is more than just something we eat—it carries memories, traditions, and stories.

I created Mangan to share some of those recipes and the stories behind them. Every dish is a small reminder of home and of the people who taught me to appreciate Filipino cuisine. Through this website, I hope to share a taste of our Filipino kitchen with you, one recipe at a time.

Mangan means “to eat” in Kapampangan, and for me, it represents more than simply eating. It represents gathering, sharing, and keeping the flavors of home alive.
